hellsite. is a Tumblr app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Read your dashboard, blogs
and tags; like, reblog and follow; write posts with photos, keep drafts,
schedule posts in your queue and answer asks. Activity shows your notes and
new followers, with notifications if you want them.

The dashboard is the one Tumblr sends, in the same order. hellsite. remembers
the last post you read, so after a day away you can jump straight back and
read up to the newest, and that place follows you from your phone to your Mac.
Calm mode hides note counts, keeps GIFs and video still until you tap them,
and holds new posts until you ask for them. Posts with content warnings you
choose, or from blogs and words you mute, fold away behind a tap.

There's a gallery for photo and video posts, a reading view for long text,
lists that read several blogs as one feed, and a shelf for posts to read
later. For your own blog there are post stats, a queue planner and a way to
fix a tag across many posts at once. On iPad and Mac the feed sits next to the
post you're reading, and each window on Mac can use its own account.

No ads, no tracking, no account with us. You sign in with Tumblr.
